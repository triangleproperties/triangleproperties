About Triangle Properties Pvt. Ltd.

Company Overview

15+ Years of Uncompromising Quality

Triangle Properties Pvt. Ltd. was founded with a singular focus: bringing complete transparency, superior civil engineering, and legal integrity to Chennai's real estate landscape.

Over the past decade and a half, we have successfully handed over more than 120+ projects ranging from boutique residential apartments in Vadapalani to sprawling sea-facing luxury villas on ECR.

Our Vision

To be Chennai's most trusted real estate developer and turnkey construction partner known for structural longevity, aesthetic innovation, and 100% legal title compliance.

Our Mission

Deliver Vastu-compliant, eco-friendly luxury homes and commercial spaces on time without cost overruns, providing complete peace of mind to every buyer and landowner.
