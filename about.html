From our humble beginnings, Triangle Properties has grown into one of Chennai's most trusted and affordable real estate developers, helping hundreds of families and investors take their first step toward owning a valuable real estate asset.

At Triangle Properties, we believe that investing in land should be simple, secure, and rewarding. Our carefully planned residential and commercial plotted developments are strategically located in Tamil Nadu's fastest-growing investment corridors, including Chennai, Tiruvallur, Chengalpattu, Kanchipuram, and ECR (East Coast Road). Every project is selected with a strong focus on strategic location, future infrastructure, legal compliance, excellent connectivity, and long-term value appreciation.

Our commitment goes far beyond selling plots. We provide end-to-end guidance throughout your property journey—from site visits and legal verification to registration and post-sale support—ensuring complete transparency, professional service, and total peace of mind. Whether you're purchasing your first residential plot, building your dream home, or making a smart investment for the future, Triangle Properties is your trusted partner every step of the way.

📍 To serve our customers better, we operate from a modern 1,000 sq. ft. corporate office in Vadapalani, Chennai, where our experienced team offers personalized consultation, project guidance, and investment support in a professional and welcoming environment.

Triangle Properties was founded by Loheshvar and Santhakumar, two passionate young professionals united by a shared vision of making real estate transparent, affordable, and accessible.

Their journey began as strangers brought together by a common dream. That dream evolved into a lifelong friendship and ultimately a strong partnership built on trust, integrity, and a shared commitment to creating value for every customer.

With pure intentions and the courage to invest everything they had in a single idea, they entered the real estate industry determined to redefine the land-buying experience. Their mission was simple—to help families and investors own legally approved, high-potential properties with complete confidence and peace of mind.

Today, under their leadership, Triangle Properties has become a trusted name in plotted developments across Chennai, Tiruvallur, Chengalpattu, and ECR, delivering thoughtfully planned residential and commercial projects that combine affordability with long-term value.

For Loheshvar and Santhakumar, success is not measured by the number of properties sold, but by the trust they earn, the relationships they build, and the dreams they help bring to life.
